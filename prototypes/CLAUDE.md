# CLAUDE.md — prototypes

**Every time a prototype review session starts, read `memory/open-questions.md` first.** It holds
cross-cutting questions raised in past reviews that don't belong to one screen. Check whether the
session at hand answers any of them.

- A question answered (by an ADR, a ticket, or the review itself) gets **removed from the file**,
  not left with a "decided" strikethrough — the decision lives in the ADR/ticket, not here.
- The file may legitimately be empty; that means every question raised so far is settled.
